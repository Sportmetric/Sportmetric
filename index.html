InnoMetrics Posting

InnoMetrics Posting is our internal tool for publishing our own product videos to the official InnoMetrics accounts on TikTok and Instagram. Our team sends a video to the tool, reviews and approves the caption and posting time, and the tool then uploads the video to our account. It is used only by the InnoMetrics team and posts only to accounts we own.
