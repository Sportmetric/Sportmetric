Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what data InnoMetrics Posting (the "Service") handles. The Service is an internal tool used only by the InnoMetrics team to post to our own social media accounts.

Data we handle

  • TikTok: after we log in with our own TikTok account, we receive an access token and basic profile information (such as the account's display name and open ID). We use these only to upload our videos to that account.
  • Instagram/Meta: an access token and our Instagram business account ID, used only to publish our Reels.
  • Videos and captions that our team sends to the Service for posting.

What we don't do

We do not collect data about TikTok or Instagram users who view our posts, we do not sell data, and we do not use the data for advertising.

Storage and retention

Tokens are stored in a private, access-controlled configuration. Videos are kept only until they are posted or rejected. You can revoke the Service's access at any time in your TikTok or Instagram app settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access or delete data concerning you. Contact us through our Skroutz store page.
